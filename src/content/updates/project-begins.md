Make America E Pluribus Unum Again began with a simple question: what would happen if the country’s oldest unifying phrase became visible in everyday life again?

## What exists now

The project has a name, a rising-sun mark, and this website. The first public materials are being prepared around the phrase **E Pluribus Unum**—“Out of many, one.”

## Next steps

The next phase is practical:

- prepare shirts that help carry the message into public spaces;
- finish downloadable postcard files that anyone can print and share;
- document where the materials travel and what conversations follow;
- keep this Updates section current as the project develops.

The project will grow slowly and openly. Updates will be added when there is meaningful progress to report.
