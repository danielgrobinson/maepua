Useful Objects is an ongoing exercise in making fewer decisions, more carefully. Each piece starts with a real need and a short list of materials already at hand.

## Designed to be understood

Fasteners remain visible. Parts that wear out can be replaced. Construction should make sense before a manual is opened.

This is not nostalgia for a simpler age of making. It is a practical argument that repairability and visual clarity often arrive together.

## In use

The collection is tested at home before it is photographed. Scratches and adjustments are recorded as part of the design rather than edited out of the story.
