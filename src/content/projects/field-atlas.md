A Field Atlas collects what conventional maps leave out: a gate that always catches the afternoon light, the first stand of reeds after rain, and the unofficial path worn into a verge.

## Mapping memory

Every entry combines a photograph, a short description, and a rough location. Precision is intentionally limited. The atlas records character and recurrence rather than coordinates.

The system is built to remain unfinished. New pages fit beside old ones, and changed places receive another entry instead of replacing the first.

## An open format

The next version will publish the page template so other people can document their own repeated routes with the same loose structure.
