Most infrastructure is successful when it disappears into routine. Quiet Infrastructure looks directly at the details designed not to be noticed.

## Evidence of care

Fresh paint on an old railing, a hand-cut patch in a fence, and a drain kept clear before the rain all reveal the human work inside an apparently ordinary system.

The project pairs wide contextual views with close studies of joints, surfaces, repairs, and labels.
