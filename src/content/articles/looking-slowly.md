Novelty is useful, but it is not the only path to attention. Familiar places can become more visually demanding because we think we already understand them.

## Return on purpose

Choose one short route and walk it at different hours. Keep one lens. Photograph fewer things. Repetition removes the pressure to collect everything and makes subtle changes easier to notice.

## Make a record, not a verdict

The first photograph does not need to be definitive. It can be a note about where the shadow falls or what might be clearer after rain.

> A photograph can be part of a question instead of the final answer.

Editing repeated visits together reveals which observations had depth and which depended only on novelty.
